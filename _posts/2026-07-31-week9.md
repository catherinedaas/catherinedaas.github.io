Week 9, July 27 - July 31

This week, the focus was on refining outputs. There was some chart editing that needed to be done and some table editing as well. We are also hearing back from some of the clinical and math experts I reached out to about what might make sense for grouping and defining norms.

We are also focusing more on connecting the board measurements to clinical messaging. I honestly think this is still unclear to me, and I am hoping that visualizing the pose estimation alongside the IMU data will help.

The good thing is that there is now a process with some scientific basis that can be generalized beyond one feature set. However, it will still take some thought and consideration to determine what that information might actually be capturing about balance.
