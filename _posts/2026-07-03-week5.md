Week 5, June 29–July 3

This was an interesting week. For most of the last two weeks, I reviewed examples from clinical lab science and rehabilitation literature to better understand how to proceed. The focus is still very much mathematical, but we are probably getting closer to being able to work on the computer vision portion of this project.
