Week 8, July 20 - July 24

The rough shape of the project has developed. I feel a little silly about it sometimes because I was able to do some of these tasks more smoothly as an undergrad with R or Stata. However, I have now automated that process.

The project now has two real phases. The first is a preliminary data exploration phase that needs to happen for all features being considered for a clinician-facing output. At this stage, we are trying to identify features that might meaningfully describe balance ability and could eventually be useful for talking about changes in individual patients. We do not yet know which features will be sensitive to change, but features that differ between the lower- and higher-fall-risk groups, vary with age, or show other clinically interpretable patterns are reasonable candidates for further consideration.

We also want to appreciate what the eventual normative values for these features might look like across age. As a preliminary approach, we are using OLS regression and the residual standard deviation to construct a central 80% band around the regression line. The exploratory tools include Spearman's rho with a heat map, histograms, QQ plots, several tables reporting measures such as means with confidence intervals, AUC with confidence intervals, Mann-Whitney U tests, ANCOVA, and scatter plots with regression lines.

Because the fall-risk groups are observational rather than randomly assigned, age and fall-risk group are not completely separable. It will therefore be difficult to fully disentangle the association between age and each board feature from differences associated with fall-risk status. However, the goal at this stage is to develop a preliminary understanding of what each feature might capture about balance ability and whether it could be clinically interpretable.

All of that needs to be considered before the final features are chosen. I have decided, hopefully for better and not worse, to spend some extra time analyzing one of our feature sets extensively, with the hope that building and refining the process now will make it much faster to evaluate the other three feature sets.
